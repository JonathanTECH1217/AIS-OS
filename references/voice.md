## Register notes

Observed from the samples below. Not rules Jonathan wrote.

- Direct and confident. States the offer and asks for the meeting.
- Specific over vague: dollar figures, square footage, rankings, dates, named products.
- Industry vocabulary used naturally: integrators, low voltage, AIA, rough in, trim out, referral channels.
- Consultative in email: answers each question in order, sets boundaries ("I would not in good practice..."), explains the reasoning.
- Punchy and scripted on the phone: short lines, permission-based opener, one clear ask.
- No em dashes. Plain paragraphs in email, not bullets.
- Sign-off: "My Best, Jonathan"

## Sample 1: cold call script

```
Pattern Interrupt 

hey name, this is a cold call

Permission

Feel free to hang up anytime, but I have an offer and it would take 60 seconds 

Establish Frame

I spent 4 years in the industry marketing, selling, and installing low voltage equipment in high end homes, doing the AIA presentations. The market is getting saturated with more integrators per square mile and the ones that are growing quicker have answered the question how do I get more high end work? 

Confirm an Identity

I was wondering if you are in charge of the marketing in the company?

Give a small offer

I book jobs for home integrators and would like to give some free value to you over a 15 minute meeting and try to sell you my services on Tuesday at 9:30

Pause

Wait for objection/yes

Give concrete next steps

If yes send email
```
