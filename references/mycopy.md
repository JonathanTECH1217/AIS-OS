Read this before drafting any email reply in Jonathan's inbox. `/inbox` reads it on every boot pass. Edit it any time; the next draft follows it. Register rules and verbatim samples live in `references/voice.md`; this file is the reusable copy itself.

## Rules that apply to every draft

- **Email copy is always Times New Roman, 12pt** (Jonathan, 2026-09-15). `scripts/proton_mail.py draft` writes every draft as plain text plus an HTML part in that face; no other font, no colors, no bold.
- Open with the first name, a comma, then the point. No "hope you're well."
- 60 to 120 words. One job per email. Cut everything not doing that job.
- No hedges: never "I think," "I believe," "probably." State it or cut it.
- No em dashes. Plain paragraphs, not bullets, in email.
- The ask is two times and a length: "Tuesday at 10:30 or Thursday at 9. Fifteen minutes."
- Never put a number or a question in the other person's mouth. The reply reflects only what they wrote.
- After a call, the email never repeats what Jonathan said on the call: his name, his background (the install years, the $91k job), what Monarc does. They just heard it. The email carries only what they asked for, the time if they booked, and at most one line in their own words (Jonathan, 2026-10-01).
- **Price line on hold since 2026-10-05** (Jonathan: "I was offering too low to the wrong people."; `context/offer.md`). No price in a new draft until the new offer is written; a price question goes under "Needs you" in the report.
- Price only if they asked. If they asked (offer locked 2026-09-15): "$2k/m retainer for a 60-day pilot. Minimum ad spend is $2k/m; for the pilot I match $1,000 of it each month, both months. $3k/m all in, $6k for the pilot. After the pilot it's $2.5k/m management plus $2k/m ad spend."
- One artifact carries the proof (a photo, a proposal, a permit), never "check the website."
- Never name PHA or link pha.systems.
- Sign-off: "My Best, Jonathan" on a first touch. "Jonathan." on anything after.
