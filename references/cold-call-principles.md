# Cold call script rules

Extracted 2026-09-07 from Jonathan's own script (`references/voice.md`, Sample 1). Apply these when drafting or reviewing any outbound script: phone, DM, or first email.

## The five rules

1. **Every line lowers threat or raises curiosity.** Nothing else earns a spot in 60 seconds.
2. **Honesty is the tactic.** Say it's a cold call. Say they can hang up. Say you're going to try to sell them. Each admission buys trust the next line spends.
3. **Specific beats smooth.** Years in the trade, AIA, Tuesday 9:30. Vague sounds like a salesman. Specific sounds like an insider.
4. **Their outcome, never your service.** Booked appointments with high ticket prospects. Not SEO, not Google Ads, not marketing.
5. **Stack small yeses and price each one.** 60 seconds to stay. A yes to confirm they're the person. 15 minutes at one named slot. Nobody is asked for anything big.

## Every line has one job

Label each line with exactly one of these. No job, cut it. Two jobs, split it.

| Job | What the line does | In the current script |
|---|---|---|
| DISARM | Name what they're about to think | "hey name, this is a cold call" |
| BOUND | Hand back control and price the cost of staying | "hang up anytime, 60 seconds" |
| PROVE | Insider credential in trade vocabulary | "4 years, low voltage, AIA presentations" |
| NAME | Their problem in their words, ending on the question they already ask themselves | "saturated, more integrators per square mile, how do I get more high end work?" |
| QUALIFY | Confirm the right person; collect a yes | "are you in charge of marketing?" |
| ASK | Outcome, small ask, honest intent, specific slot | "I book jobs... 15 minutes... try to sell you... Tuesday 9:30" |
| SILENCE | Stop talking after the ask | Pause |
| LOCK | Put the yes in writing immediately | "if yes, send email" |

## Hard limits

- Under 45 seconds read aloud, so a 60 second promise is kept with room.
- Every ASK line has three written objections under it with a one-sentence return to the ask.
- No em dashes. Short lines. Plain words.
- Never impersonate Jonathan on a live call or send in his name without a draft approved first.
