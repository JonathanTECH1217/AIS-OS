# The free-campaign call (the second window)

Drafted 2026-10-03 for the campaign "Free campaign for a review" (`projects/free-campaign/README.md`). Not approved until Jonathan says so. Same shape as the cold call in `references/voice.md` (Sample 1), the rules in `references/cold-call-principles.md`: one job per line, every line lowers threat or raises curiosity, honesty is the tactic, their outcome never our service, one small ask, three objections under the ASK, the yes put in writing. Under 45 seconds read aloud. The dial hour keeps the meeting ask; this script runs in its own window and its own log (Prospects, "Free campaign calls").

What he says up front, in his words: it is free, he will ask for a Google review after, and then he will try to sell them on us running it.

## The script

| Job | Line |
|---|---|
| DISARM | Hey {name}, this is a cold call. |
| BOUND | Hang up anytime. I have something free and it takes 45 seconds to say. |
| PROVE | I spent four years installing low voltage in high end homes and did the AIA talks. Now I pull the search results in a town and show the integrator who a buyer finds. |
| NAME | Most shops I call say their work is referrals. Then I search their city and they're not there. The buyer who searched hired someone else. |
| QUALIFY | Are you the one who decides on marketing? |
| ASK | Here's the offer. I pull the three searches a {city} buyer types, show you who shows up and where you sit, and build you a Google Ads campaign for {service} you can load yourself, paused. Free. After you've had a look I'll ask you for a Google review. Then I'll try to sell you on us running it. What email do I send it to? |
| SILENCE | Stop. Wait. |
| LOCK | Got it, {email}. It lands in a day or two. The review ask comes after, no strings. |

Honest about all three in one breath: free, the review, the sale. Nobody is asked for anything until the files are in their inbox.

## Three objections under the ASK

1. **"What's the catch?"** No catch. The files are yours whether you review or not, and whether you ever call me again. I'm betting the check shows you something worth a call. Email?
2. **"We don't do Google Ads."** Then the campaign sits paused and the search check still shows who a buyer finds instead of you. That part is worth the read on its own. Email?
3. **"Just send it to info@."** I can. It'll sit. Whose inbox does the marketing land in? That one.

## After the call

- Type the outcome in the "Free campaign calls" row the way you do on Contacts: "yes", "send it", "no", "call back Tuesday", "wrong vertical". Put the email in Email. A yes is what builds the delivery.
- The AIOS runs `python scripts/free_campaign.py queue` and the delivery draft lands in Proton Drafts for you to send (the first three after your word on the row).
