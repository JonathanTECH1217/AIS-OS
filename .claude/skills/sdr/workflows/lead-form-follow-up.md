# Lead form submission follow-up

Workflow of the SDR skill. Status: copy not drafted. Next after cold outreach is approved.

One line: Answers every form fill on the site and keeps writing until the lead answers, books, or says stop.

A response workflow: the lead moved first. Not the same thing as appointment booking (Jonathan, 2026-10-05), which takes over once the lead says yes or asks for a time.

## Objective

No form fill goes cold. Every one gets a first reply and a sequence, and the ones that answer are handed on.

## What sets it off

A form is filled out on the company's site. At Monarc: a booking or a form fill from monarcbuild.com (`scripts/avmarketing-booking.gs`, `templates/booking-drip.md`).

## Steps

1. **Intake:** every form on the site and where each one lands; what the first reply says; how many touches and over how many days; what takes a lead out (answered, booked, said no, said stop); the minutes inside which the first reply must go.
2. **Wire every form** so each fill is one row on the record with its source.
3. **The copy:** the first reply, the sequence, and the last touch. He approves it (the pass).
4. **Build it** in the company's own tools. A reply, a booking, or a stop ends the sequence at once.
5. **A rehearsal:** ten test fills from the owner's own addresses.
6. **Hand over** an answer to the right workflow: a yes or a time goes to appointment booking; a question is drafted and waits.
7. **Monthly check:** fills in, first-reply time, touches sent, answers by sort, booked, stops.
