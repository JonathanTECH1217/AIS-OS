# Stale proposal follow-up

Workflow of the SDR skill. Status: copy not drafted.

One line: Goes back to every proposal that went out and got no answer.

## Objective

No proposal dies in silence. Every one that goes quiet gets asked about until there is a yes, a no, or a reason.

## What sets it off

A proposal or terms email went out and no answer came back by the day set at intake. At Monarc: a deal at Proposed on the CRM board with its next-action date passed (`projects/crm/playbook.json`, the Proposed steps).

## Steps

1. **Intake:** where proposals are sent from and logged; how many days of silence make one stale; how many touches and how far apart; what ends it (signed, said no, a reason logged).
2. **Find the stale ones** from the record each morning.
3. **The copy:** touch 1, touch 2, and the last one that asks for a no. He approves it (the pass).
4. **Send** on the same thread as the proposal. A reply ends the sequence and goes to him.
5. **Log** the close reason when one is given. It is what the coach reads later.
6. **Monthly check:** proposals out, gone stale, answered after a touch, signed, closed with a reason.
