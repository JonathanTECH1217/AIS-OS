## Report 1: six syllables, eight heads

- Dots draw again. `addDots` uses the 3.x call (`addDotToAll`) when the 4.x one is missing, for notes and for rests, and warns once in the console if neither exists. Before: 0 of 245 dotted values in the sweep had a dot.
- Ties between two heads a sixteenth apart run from head centre to head centre, a little lower. Before: 0.44 px wide. Sweep now: 591 ties, all 12.8 px or wider, 0 head mismatches.
- A blind spread (song not heard) starts 0.15 s after the stamp, 0.45 s apart at most, and its onsets snap to eighths. Before: 3-slot steps at 100 bpm, every second note split into tied heads. Blurry blind fill: about 540 heads for 480 records, was about 600.

## Report 2: the playhead skips

- Play, seek and loop wrap: the band waits at its start slot until Spotify's first reading of the new spot (4 s at most), then runs. Before: it ran ahead on the estimate and jumped back 280 to 460 ms two seconds in, every loop round.
- The clock estimate is the second-largest of the last five offsets (readings can only be late), and three strays in a row are needed before the clock moves. A big disagreement must show twice before the band jumps; one alone only pulls it at the absorbing rate. Before: two stale readings moved the band twice by 420 ms.
- Absorb is measured from the last frame, not the last correction, at 0.1 s per s. Before: a 200 ms step in one frame after an idle stretch.
- A poll answered after Stop, Play or a seek is dropped; Stop no longer leaves the song "playing". Polls stay at 500 ms until three readings are in.
- The listener's 5 s re-lock is thrown away when it would move the grid under half a slot and the tempo under 2 %. The listener's clock pairs reset on Play, seek and song change; a new capture session (new t0) empties the old frames.
- Auto-scroll follows the red line up as well as down; a scroll the band did not write itself counts as a hand scroll.

Test `ph-band.js`: snaps none in every case (start, one and two stale readings, 30 % stale, loop wrap); largest per-frame move 16 ms; absorb after a quiet spell 2 ms in one frame.
