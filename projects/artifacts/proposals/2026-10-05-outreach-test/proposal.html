Outreach

New conversations with the people you most want to reach, every week.

Prepared for Jonathan Beach, Monarc Build.

The outcome

Every week your company starts new conversations with the people it most wants to reach. Every booked meeting lands on your calendar with where it came from. You build no list and write no first message.

What we put in place

  1. Intake. One sitting, seven facts: who to reach, where, what marks a good one, the outcome you offer, your proof, the ask, and who answers replies.
  2. The list. Companies that fit, pulled from Google Maps. For each one: the owner's name, a verified email, a LinkedIn profile, and the reason it is on the list. You check 20 rows before anything goes out.
  3. The messages. A first message and two follow-ups for each channel, in your words. You approve every one.
  4. The sending. Set up in your own accounts, with a daily cap. It stops the moment someone replies.
  5. A rehearsal. Ten sends to your own addresses before a prospect sees one.
  6. The record. Every touch, reply, and booking is logged with its source. Anyone who says stop is never contacted again.
  7. The hand-off. One page: where the list is, how to pause, how to add rows, how to change a message.
  8. The monthly check. We read the numbers, change one message, top up the list, and send you one page.

How we both know it works

Before the first real send

  • 18 of the 20 rows you check are right.
  • Every message is approved by you.
  • 10 of 10 test sends reach the inbox.

After 30 days

  • Every reply was sorted within one business day.
  • Bounces under 3 in 100. Spam complaints under 1 in 1,000.
  • Booked meetings a week at or over the number we set together at intake.
  • Every booked meeting carries its source.

What we need from you

  • The seven facts, and your do-not-contact list.
  • Access to the sending mailbox, the LinkedIn seat, the calendar, and your CRM. A grant or an invite, never a password.
  • Your check of 20 rows, and your approval of each message.
  • A reply to every interested lead within one business day.

What this does not promise

No one can promise a number of meetings or closed jobs. Those depend on your market and your follow-up. The weekly target is set with you at intake and read every month.
