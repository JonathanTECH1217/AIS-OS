Integrator seed list, built September 13, 2026 by Monarc Build's AIOS.

WHAT IS IN THE ZIP
- places-seed-2026-09-13.xlsx: the raw sweep. 10,077 Google listings of home integrators, AV companies, and home
  theater builders. Sheets: Integrators, Extras, Method.
- qualified-seed-2026-09-13.xlsx: the same companies after each website was read and sorted into tiers
  (residential vs commercial, the brands they carry, site quality). 1,934 rows. Sheets: Qualified, Excluded, Unverified, All scored, Method.
- this file.

HOW IT WAS BUILT
- Google Places text search over 213 US locations: the largest metros plus the towns where $2M to $25M homes cluster.
- Six searches per metro: home automation installer, home theater installation, audio video integrator,
  smart home integrator, lutron dealer, control4 dealer.
- Google returns at most 60 results per search, so this is the top of each market, not every company in it.
- Rows already on Jonathan's own 2,000-row list are marked In Repository = yes.

COLUMNS (raw sweep)
Name, Address, State, County, Phone, Local Time @ 12pm ET (their clock when it is noon in Annapolis), Reviews
(rating and count), Website, Status, Outcome, then the Google extras: Place ID, Maps URL, primary type, business
status, domain, source list, DMV flag (DC, MD, VA), City, Zip, Rating, Review count, and the search that found it.
