# Monarc Build style guide (monarcbuild.com)

Governs every page on monarcbuild.com. `/landing-page` reads this file first, before any build, render, or drift check. Edit this file to change the site; the next build follows it.

- **Checkable** rules are what the drift check tests. Each has a number or an exact value.
- **Pending** rules are Jonathan's to settle. Resume point and open questions: `brainstorms/2026-09-13-landing-page-sop.md` (resume at Q24). A pending rule has a render placeholder so builds can proceed; the placeholder is not a decision.

Started by Jonathan 2026-09-12. Made checkable 2026-09-13 from the grill-me interview. His original notes are preserved at the bottom, unchanged.

**Two layers (Jonathan, 2026-09-25).** "My spacing rules and ratios should remain the same but as far as color and typography I want it to match the channel that it is targetted at." From 2026-09-25 the guide has an invariant layer and a theme layer. The invariants hold on every page: layout and spacing (section 3), contrast (Color 3), the copy rules (section 4), media honesty (section 5), motion, the ad-page bones (section 6), and the legal pages (section 7). The theme layer is one file per look in `themes/` (section 8): palette, faces and weights, the two button labels, button shape, card style, section-label style, the hero render. The homepage and `/av_marketing/` follow the **house** theme, which is sections 1 and 2 as they were written; each service page of the Google Ads plan (`projects/google-ads/`, Section Spec "Service pages") follows its own theme. A page names its theme with `<meta name="mb-theme" content="<name>">`; `drift.py` reads the palette, the button color, the allowed weights, and the labels from that file. Where a rule below is theme-scoped it says so; everything else is invariant.

## 1. Color

**Theme-scoped (2026-09-25).** The table below is the house theme (`themes/house.json`). A service page's table is its theme file. Rules 1, 2, and 4 are read against the page's theme; rule 3 is invariant on every page and every theme file carries its measured pairs.

| Name | Hex | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Oak | `#61534E` | Primary button, and nothing else | Confirmed (Q6) |
| Charcoal Blue | `#2F334D` | Headline ink (h1, h2, h3); the hero scrim and the services section ground on the ad page (Jonathan, 2026-09-23), with the block's label, h2, and sub in White | Confirmed (Q6) |
| Charcoal | `#4B4C5C` | Body text | Pending. Render placeholder. |
| Stone | `#6E6E78` | Labels, captions, nav, footer text | Pending. Render placeholder. |
| Sand | `#C2B3A9` | Alternating section band, image frames | Pending. Render placeholder. |
| Paper | `#F6F3EF` | Page ground | Pending. Assistant placeholder, not one of Jonathan's five. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Button text, card ground, hero text on the photo scrim (ad page, Jonathan 2026-09-23) | Pending. Render placeholder. |

Rules:
1. **Checkable.** The theme's button color appears on the buttons only (house: Dark Oak on the primary button and nothing else). No other element uses it. Exception (Jonathan, 2026-09-23): on the ad page's service cards, the icons, the drawings, and every word (title, line, label, hint) are Dark Oak on White, about 7.2:1 (section 6 rule 15).
2. **Checkable.** No hex value outside the page's theme file appears in the page CSS. (Pure white, the popup scrim black, and the theme's palette are the whole set.)
3. **Checkable, invariant.** Body text on its ground meets WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for headings 24px and up and for icons that carry meaning. Every theme file lists its measured pairs. House: Charcoal `#4B4C5C` on Paper `#F6F3EF` is about 7.6:1. Charcoal Blue on Sand is about 6.1:1. Stone on Sand is about 2.9:1, so Stone is not body text on Sand. Charcoal on Sand is about 4.1:1 (measured 2026-09-19), so body text and labels on a Sand band are set in Charcoal Blue. A theme color that passes only at 3:1 (the Google page's green and red, the social page's green on its grey ground) sits on 24px headings and on check and cross marks, never on body lines.
4. One accent. No second saturated color anywhere, including icons, rules, and the step-7 loop mark (drawn in Charcoal Blue or Stone). **Theme-scoped:** a theme may name more than one accent (the Google page's four colors; every service page's fit green and red); the theme file lists them and nothing outside the list appears.
5. **Pending (Q24+).** Page ground: white, Paper, Sand, or alternating bands. Body text color.

## 2. Typography

**Theme-scoped (2026-09-25).** Rules 1, 2, 4, and 6 describe the house theme; a service page takes its faces, weights, and label style from its theme file. Rule 5's size scale is invariant.

1. **Checkable.** Two faces. A serif for h1, h2, h3, and the hero sub headline. A sans for body, labels, nav, buttons, footer. (A theme may name one face or two; the theme file says which.) The wordmark in the Monarc Build lockup (Outfit 500, 2026-09-26) is drawn as outlines inside the mark's SVG, so it loads no font and is not a page face.
2. **Checkable.** Weights: regular (400) and italic (400) only. No bold, semibold, or black anywhere. Hierarchy comes from size and letterspacing. (A theme may allow more: the commercial theme allows 700; `drift.py` reads the list.)
3. Labels (section eyebrows, step numbers) are sans, small, tracked about 0.12em, in Stone. (Theme-scoped: each theme file names its label style.)
4. **Pending.** Exact faces. Render placeholders: Cormorant Garamond (serif) and Inter (sans), both from Google Fonts with Georgia and system-ui fallbacks. Jonathan picks from rendered samples. Service-page faces are set per theme (section 8), all from Google Fonts.
5. **Pending.** Size scale and line heights. Render placeholders at 1440: h1 56px/1.1, h2 40px/1.15, h3 24px/1.25, body 18px/1.6, sub 22px/1.4, label 13px. At 390: h1 36px, h2 30px, body 17px. Ad page placeholders (Jonathan, 2026-09-23), at 1024px and up: h1 64px/1.05 with -0.015em tracking, hero sub 24px, proof figures 72px with lining numerals; phone sizes unchanged. The same scale holds on every theme.
6. Section labels that open a block are centered and carry a 40px hairline on each side, drawn in CSS in the label's own color (Jonathan, 2026-09-23, from `references/design-references.md` entry 1). Never typed dash characters. (House theme; other themes name their own label style.)

## 3. Layout

**Invariant.** Spacing, the column, the ratios, and the edge rule hold on every page and every theme (Jonathan, 2026-09-25). Rule 2 has a dated service-page exception; rule 5's shadow line is theme-scoped.

1. **Checkable.** Content column is two thirds of the viewport: `max-width: 960px`, centered. Below 768px the column is full width with 24px side padding. Jonathan's "67% filled" rule means this (Q10).
2. **Checkable.** Hero: headline, sub headline, then the VSL, stacked and centered (Q5, Jonathan's note). **Service-page exception (Jonathan, 2026-09-25, pages capture Q9):** on a service page the hero is split, the headline, sub, one line of copy where the page needs it, and the hero button on the left, the theme's 3D render on the right; on phones the two stack with the render under the sub. The house pages keep the stacked hero. **Email page exception (Jonathan, 2026-09-26):** the putt render runs full width and the headline, sub, and button sit over its beige sky, in ink on cream. **Automation page (same day):** the house render is a sticker, the house and its slab cut out with a white border and a soft shadow, set on the dark ground. **Homepage (Jonathan, 2026-09-26 and 27, `brainstorms/2026-09-26-homepage-google-rebuild.md`):** the stacked VSL hero is retired on `/`. Round 3 (2026-09-27, "the google type and copy but not a google search results page"): a Google product-site layout, the hero centered (an eyebrow in plain tracked caps, its four hue dots off since 2026-09-27, the h1, the sub, the search bar typing "marketing for contractors", the button), then the six services as render cards, the three jobs, Who it is for and Who it is not for, about, the questions, and the closing ask (theme `monarc`, section 8). Rounds 1 (a Google results page) and 2 (the same in a warm theme) were retired unshipped.
3. **Pending (Q23, render approval).** Alignment of the six non-hero blocks. Variations rendered 2026-09-13 in `renders/`: A centered headings with split bodies, B everything centered, C alternating 50/50 with left-aligned headings.
4. **Pending (Q24+).** Vertical rhythm. Render placeholders: 96px section padding top and bottom, 56px between a section's heading block and its body, 24px inside a stack.
5. Nothing touches a viewport edge. No drop shadows. No boxes around text unless it is a card in Upsell, a case card, or a service card on the ad page (section 6 rules 7 and 15, 2026-09-23). **Theme-scoped (2026-09-25):** a theme may allow one kind of shadow on its cards (the commercial theme's hard offset, the social theme's soft 1px); the theme file names it, and the house theme allows none.
6. Media boxes are fixed ratio: VSL 16:9, case tiles 4:3, founder photo 4:5, portfolio 16:10. Images fill with `object-fit: cover`.

## 4. Copy

**Invariant** on every page and every theme, with rule 6 reading its labels from the theme.

1. **Checkable.** One h1 per page, 8 words max. Every h2 8 words max. Every sub headline (the first paragraph after an h1 or h2) 25 words max.
2. **Checkable.** No em dashes or en dashes anywhere on the page, including alt text and meta tags.
3. **Checkable.** No tenure claim: no "N years," no "since 20XX," no "decade" (Q19).
4. **Checkable.** No tier prices on the page: `$2,500` and `$4,500` never appear. ~~Proof numbers ($70k, $91k, $50k, $500k+, $2.5k of ads) are allowed (decision 2026-09-08, Q3).~~ **No dollar figures on the jobs, on any page (Jonathan, 2026-09-27: "Take the number figures out of the jobs", then "remove the figures and keep scope"):** a job shows its name, its photo, and its scope (whole home audio, Lutron Ketra lighting, a Control4 and Lutron retrofit) and where the lead came from (a ranked page, Google Ads), never the job's value or the ad spend. The "Page 1" and "#1 page" ranking claims stay. The math block on `/av_marketing/` (the ticket and margin math) is the offer, not a job, and stays.
5. **Checkable.** Never name PHA, Performance Home Automation, or link pha.systems (rule 2026-09-09).
6. **Checkable.** One button label on the whole page: "Book an appointment" (Jonathan, 2026-09-23). No "Apply now," no second wording. From 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-23 the label was "Book a 15 minute call"; the homepage still carries it until Jonathan asks for the change, and `drift.py` allows that one old label until then. Step buttons inside a booking popup may be labeled exactly "OK", "Back", "Send", or "Confirm appointment" (the last added 2026-09-23 for the calendar step) and must carry `data-step-btn`; this holds on every page, not only ad pages (Jonathan, 2026-09-19: the homepage button opens the same popup, first name, email, phone, then a time). **Service pages (Jonathan, 2026-09-25, pages capture Q7 and Q8):** two labels per page, set in the theme file: the hero label on the hero button and on the floating pill (two instances), the final-ask label on the final ask (one instance). Both open the same popup. `drift.py` counts two and one.
7. Trade vocabulary is allowed: rough-in, spec, high ticket, Lutron, Ketra, Control4, James Loudspeaker. The reader is the contractor who typed the search (Q15; widened past integrators 2026-09-25). **Checkable (Jonathan, 2026-09-25).** Reading level is measured by `readability.py` with brand names and trade terms whitelisted (`readability-exempt.txt`), so the score reads sentence length and everyday-word syllables only: every block at or under Flesch-Kincaid grade 8, headlines and subs aimed at 6, no sentence over 25 words. A trade term or an acronym is glossed in plain words the first time it appears on a page ("AEO, showing up in AI answers"; "Google Business Profile, the map listing"). Client sites keep their seventh-grade rule. ~~No reading-level rule (2026-09-13 to 2026-09-25).~~
8. Register per `references/voice.md`: short sentences, numbers, named products, no fluff.
9. **Checkable.** No callback-time promise anywhere on a page (Jonathan, 2026-09-23: "no promises on 5 minute callback windows"). No "within five minutes," no "inside five minutes," no "five minute callback," no "same day." Speed is described, never clocked. `drift.py` flags a call, reply, text, or answer paired with a minute, second, or hour figure. On the automation page this covers the missed-call text-back: "a missed call gets a text back" is allowed, a clock is not.
10. **Service pages (Jonathan, 2026-09-25).** The h1 mirrors the ad group's target term from the keyword research (`projects/google-ads/keywords/`) in 8 words or fewer, and the sub carries the outcome in 20 or fewer. This is a dated exception to cold-call rule 4 ("their outcome, never your service"): the searcher already named the service, and the page has two seconds to confirm they are in the right place.
11. **The eyebrow names the person (Jonathan, 2026-10-01: "the eyebrow should always be naming the person this is targeted toward").** A hero eyebrow names who the page is for, in their terms: "Marketing for AV integrators" on `/av_marketing/`, "For the trades" on `/`. Never the section, the service, or the company. Not met yet: `/about/`'s eyebrow reads "About", and the six service pages carry no eyebrow; both are Jonathan's call.
