The skill reads this after the Style Guide and before it builds or edits any page. It adds a line in the same turn he changes something. The Style Guide stays the rulebook for monarcbuild.com; a line here that he confirms and that can be checked moves there, dated.

## How to read a line

- **What he did or said**: his words, or the change he made, and the page it was on.
- **The rule drawn**: the AIOS's reading of what that means for the next page. It is a reading until the last column says confirmed.
- **Standing**: `heard` (said once, not yet used), `reading` (the AIOS drew a rule from a change), `confirmed` (he said yes), `one page only` (he struck it as a general rule).

## Big misses he wants named

Jonathan, 2026-10-05: "Adjustments should also be recommended when there's big misses that could increase ... conversion rate heavily."

A miss is big when it stands between the visitor and the ask. The AIOS's starting list, his to edit:

- The ask is missing, buried below the first screen, or says different things in different places.
- The headline does not say what is offered or who it is for.
- The form asks for more than it needs.
- The proof is far from the ask, or is not real.
- The page is slow, broken, or hard to read on a phone.

The skill names at most three, the costliest first, each with where it is on the page and why it costs leads. Small polish is left out. A big miss is recommended, never fixed without his go.
