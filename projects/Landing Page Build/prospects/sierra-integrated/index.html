Our process

Discovery and consultation

We begin with a lighting design consultation to understand your home, lifestyle, and goals: plans, room usage, aesthetic preferences, and any smart home integration needs.

Conceptual design

A lighting concept that balances function, atmosphere, and architectural intent. Fixture types, layering, and control approaches are outlined for each space.

Detailed lighting plan

Fixture placement, beam spreads, control zones, and performance requirements, so builders and electricians have clarity.

Installation support and final tuning

We support the installation and fine-tune lighting scenes, dimming, and controls for a polished final result.

Questions we get asked

Do I really need a lighting designer if I already have an electrician or architect?

Electricians and architects play important roles, but a lighting design specialist focuses specifically on light quality, layering, and performance to enhance the final experience.

Can you work from my existing blueprints or during a remodel?

Yes. We regularly design lighting for both new construction and remodels using existing plans.

How long does the lighting design process usually take?

Timelines vary by project size, but most lighting design services align with the early planning and construction phases.

Can you work with fixtures I've already purchased?

When possible, we can incorporate existing fixtures while ensuring compatibility and performance.

Will the lighting system be easy for my family to use?

Absolutely. We design lighting controls to be intuitive, with simple keypads, scenes, and app-based access.

What areas do you service?

We provide lighting design services throughout Reno, Tahoe, Truckee, Carson City, Incline Village, Crystal Bay, and other surrounding areas of Nevada.
