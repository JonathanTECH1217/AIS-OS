What lighting control gives you

Keypads with your scenes

Warm white in the morning and at night, brighter and cooler by midday. The color of the light shifts with your body clock, so the house feels right at every hour.

About us

Gold Standard Integration is a family business working to provide the best electrical contractor service in Palm Beach County. Lighting control systems, EV charging stations, and panel upgrades, from the first design consultation to the install.

For architects, builders, and designers

Our process

Design consultation

We walk the plans with you and settle which rooms get keypads, where each one sits, and what every scene does.

Lighting plan

Fixtures, loads, and keypad layouts on paper before the ceilings close, so nothing is guessed on site.

Wiring

Our own electricians run the wire on your schedule, alongside the other trades, so no wall is opened twice.

Installation and programming

Keypads in, scenes set, and a walkthrough with the homeowner once the house is lived in.
