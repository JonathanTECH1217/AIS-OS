## What runs through all fifteen

- **Proof is the common gap.** Most of their sites state no Google rating and show no reviews, so the star banner he asks for under the button cannot be drawn from their site. One Google profile pull a company (Places API, about 4 cents, 15 companies about 60 cents) fills the rating, five reviews, and ten photos. It waits on his go.
- **Real photos are the second gap.** Several sites run AI-made or vendor marketing pictures; those were left off, never used as their work.
- **No calendar anywhere.** Every form ends on the marked "calendar opens here" box.
- **Facts kept honest.** Nothing invented: no rating, year, award, or count that is not on their own site. Where his Loom named a service or a city their site does not support, the brief says what was used instead.
