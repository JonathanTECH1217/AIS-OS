What lighting control does for your home

Control

Run each room on its own. Dim the living room and keep the bedroom dark, from your phone or by asking.

Automation

Lights follow the time of day and who is in the room. Movie night, dinner, and reading are one tap each.

Security

Check and switch your lights when you are away. In an emergency they can flash or go to full bright.
