## Where every fact came from

- Logo, orange (`#F04E31`), faces (Montserrat, PT Sans), phone, address, the four "neighbors" points, the five "Momentum Difference" words, and the reviews (Jeff P., Amy R., Stephanie M., Dan S.): their public site, read 2026-10-05.
- "5.0 on Google from 32 reviews": the Google Maps pull of 2026-09-24 (`projects/outreach/electrician-list-2026-09-24.csv`). Check it before anyone outside sees the page.
- The three card lines: shortened from the feature list on their page. Nothing about a keypad or a brand of gear: their page names neither.
- Top menu removed on purpose: one page, one service, one thing to do.
- The form sends nothing. It is a render.

## Big misses the AIOS would raise (his to take or leave)

1. **No photos of their lighting work.** The portfolio boxes are empty and the hero is a street photo. For a high-ticket home job, real rooms they did are the proof a buyer looks for. Ask them for five photos.
2. **The reviews are not about lighting.** One is about a wall oven on their site, and none name lighting control. The four used here are the most general. Two reviews from lighting clients would carry more than all six.
3. **The form has no calendar.** He asked for a booking link at the end. Until they have one, the page promises a call back, and a lead that waits goes cold.
