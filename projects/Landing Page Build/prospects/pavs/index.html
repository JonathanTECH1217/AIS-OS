Questions we get asked

What is a keypad?

A wall panel with a few labeled buttons in place of a bank of switches. One press sets every light in the room to a scene.

Can I run the lights by voice or from my phone?

Yes. The system ties into an app and to voice control, so you can set a scene from the couch or from the airport.

Does this work in a home that is already built?

Yes. Wireless dimmers and keypads go into an existing home, and a new build gets wired during rough-in.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The keypads and dimmers keep working on the wall. Only control from away needs a connection.
