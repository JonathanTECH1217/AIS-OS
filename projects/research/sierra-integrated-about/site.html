Trusted Smart Home & AV Experts Serving Reno, Tahoe & Truckee

Our people and the services we provide define our firm. We take pride in delivering a first-class client experience.

Who We Are

Sierra Integrated Systems is a service-oriented company built on performance and integrity. The company was founded in 2002 and focuses on high-end residential and commercial low-voltage contracting and lighting control. As a trusted AV company in Reno, NV and the greater Lake Tahoe area, we offer a broad spectrum of services utilizing the latest in technology; you imagine the perfect environment for your Reno, Incline Village, or Tahoe City home, and we make it all happen.

Enjoy expertly designed lighting control and Lutron motorized shades, high-quality music and movies available in your favorite rooms. Have a home that is healthy, secure, connected, and protected, even when you are away. Whether you're seeking a reliable home automation company for your primary residence in Reno or a vacation property in Crystal Bay, our experienced team is dedicated to your total satisfaction from Nevada to California.

From creative, integrated design, project management, and installation of the most innovative home automation and AV products, Sierra Integrated Systems stands apart from other home automation companies in Reno, NV. We connect and support your smart home systems with professionalism and precision. We collaborate with you, your architect, builder, and designer to make your vision come to life.

Our Mission

We value trust—from our clients and between our team members—above all else. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of service, performance, and integrity. We take pride in being both the best at our work and the best people to work with. Our company culture rewards proficiency and productivity just as much as teamwork and relationships.

Why Us

We know you have options when choosing an electrical contractor or technology integrator. So why choose Sierra Integrated Systems? By partnering with us, you can depend on a professional, hassle-free installation as well as dedicated hands-on project management.

Our tried-and-true process begins by meeting with you to discuss your requirements so that we can make informed decisions that allow us to design systems curated just for you. The valuable information you provide will be the basis of a design for your system, which will include your priorities and budget goals. Our all-inclusive bids aim to create fewer surprises and unexpected costs. Our experienced designers and project managers will go over options such as AV installation, audio optimization, motorized shading, media rooms or home theaters, and smart lighting design, just to name a few.

When considering lighting, most will seek the help of a professional lighting designer. Lighting designers can add a considerable cost to any project, which is why at Sierra Integrated Systems, we have partnered with Light Can Help You (LCHY) to offer our clients an in-house lighting design option at substantial savings. The goal of a professional lighting design is to achieve the right mood in each room, as well as enhance aesthetics by highlighting architectural features, artwork, etc. It all starts with an initial meeting with one of LCHY’s professional lighting designers to find out what benefits you want to accomplish. Then it’s up to the experts to craft a layered lighting design by selecting the right fixtures and loads. Early consideration in the design phase of your home will define the scope for your electrical subcontractor.

After the finalization of each project, we offer ongoing support services and optional 24/7 monitoring. At Sierra Integrated Systems, we hold our customers in the highest regard and always strive to deliver professional, honest, and dedicated service. Whether you're investing in smart home integration in Reno or planning a full AV system upgrade, our team is here to guide you from start to finish.

Proudly serving Carson City, Reno, Lake Tahoe, Truckee, and surrounding communities, Sierra Integrated Systems is your trusted partner for AV installation, lighting design, and smart home integration across Northern Nevada and California.

Keith Burrowes

Keith Burrowes is the Managing Member of Sierra Integrated Systems, LLC (SIS), which he founded in 2002. Previously, he was President and Chief Executive Officer of LECTRIX LLC and LECTRIX International BV, jointly owned by American Electric Power, Bechtel, and Siemens. This company developed markets in power quality and reliability for data centers and other industrial customers globally and did merchant transmission projects in North America. Earlier, he had a total of 28 years’ experience in positions of increasing responsibility at Bechtel, a leading international engineering and construction company. During his Bechtel career, he held positions of Manager of Engineering for Bechtel Power Corporation, Corporate Vice President and Manager of Information Systems and Services, and President of Bechtel Software, Inc.

After graduating from UCLA with his Master’s in Mechanical Engineering, he joined Bechtel’s Power Division and started his career designing nuclear generating stations, while concurrently completing his MBA at Golden Gate University. One of his assignments was Project Engineer responsible for the design and licensing of the Hope Creek Generating Station, a $4 billion nuclear power plant in southern New Jersey.

Along with SIS, he is the past Chair of the Board of Economic Development Authority of Western Nevada (EDAWN) and past Chair of the Board of the Reno Philharmonic Association.

Keith was recognized by Nevada Business Magazine as a 2024 Most Respected Leader and received certificates of special congressional recognition from the Nevada senator, governor, lieutenant governor, and others.
