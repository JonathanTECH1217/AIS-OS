Reliant Smart Home Evolution Plan

A complimentary upgrade roadmap for every Reliant customer.

Your Smart Home Deserves a Plan We Provide It at No Cost

As technology evolves, so should your home. Smart devices, lighting systems, controllers, networks, and touchscreens all have lifespans. Some reach end-of-life after 8–10 years, while others phase out when new operating systems release.

That’s why every Reliant customer receives the complimentary SmartHome Evolution Plan — a proactive, personalized roadmap that ensures your home automation system remains reliable, secure, and ready for the future.

Serving the entire Central Valley, Reliant brings professional smart home upgrade planning to communities throughout Fresno, Clovis, and Madera. Our experts ensure your system remains compatible, secure, and ready for the next generation of home automation technology.

No fees. No subscription. No obligation.
Just expert guidance and long-term support.

Why We Offer This – at No Cost

Reliant believes home technology should grow with you. The SmartHome Evolution Plan provides:

Peace of Mind

Know what in your system is aging, what’s still strong, and what may need updating in the future.

Proactive Reliability

We help prevent failures instead of waiting for equipment to break.

Future Compatibility

Ensure your system remains fully functional as Control4 releases new OS versions and retires older hardware.

Long-Term Partnership

We support our customers for the lifetime of their home technology—not just during installation.

What’s Included (Complimentary)

Annual Smart Home Health Assessment

Every year, a Reliant technician evaluates:

  • Controllers (Core/EA/HC series lifecycle position)
  • Lighting devices (Gen 1, Gen 2, Gen 3 status)
  • Touchscreens (T3 vs T4 upgrade needs)
  • Zigbee network health
  • Firmware & OS compatibility
  • Media distribution equipment
  • Security devices (locks, cameras, sensors)

You receive a clear, easy-to-understand report outlining current system health and upcoming lifecycle milestones.

Personalized Multi-Year Upgrade Roadmap

Your complimentary roadmap outlines:

  • Which devices are approaching end-of-life
  • Recommended upgrade windows
  • Opportunities to improve performance
  • Options to expand or modernize your system
  • Budget-friendly, phased scheduling over several years

This keeps you ahead of EOL changes—especially important with Control4’s evolving OS platforms (OS3 → X4).

Complimentary End-of-Life (EOL) Monitoring

We track EOL announcements and OS compatibility shifts for you.


If a component in your home is nearing retirement, Reliant will notify you and provide options long before it becomes a problem.
