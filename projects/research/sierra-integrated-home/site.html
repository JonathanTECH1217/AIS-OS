Our Design & Installation Process

We begin with a personal consultation to learn how you use your living space. This helps us build a plan aligned with your lifestyle and budget, giving you full control over your environment across Reno and Tahoe, with service also available in Truckee.

Next, our design team creates detailed equipment plans and completes the wiring and programming for each interface. Our hands-on approach ensures every system operates dependably right from day one.

Following setup, we provide continuous remote management along with comprehensive service plans to keep your installation running smoothly. Contact our team to learn more about the smart technology systems available for your home.

Explore Our Solutions

Smart Home Technology

Take complete command of your living space using custom touchscreens and keypads, with mobile control available from your phone. With Lutron & Control4 home automation systems in Nevada, you can manage entertainment and climate settings while keeping security controls close at hand. Plus, our team provides dedicated support throughout Reno and Truckee, so you’re never left to troubleshoot on your own.

Lighting Control

Custom Lutron lighting control makes managing your home simple. Program automated scenes like Welcome Home, Movie Night, or Goodnight, and adjust fixtures from wall keypads or your phone, whether you are in Crystal Bay or traveling away.

Motorized Shading

Automate your Lutron motorized shades to adjust in response to changing sunlight or your daily schedule. For mountain properties, automated window treatments help protect interior furnishings against harsh UV rays while managing heat gain. They can also provide added privacy when the home is unoccupied.

Audio Video

Enjoy rich multi-room audio and crisp video distribution tailored to every space in your residence. Our complete system design and calibration deliver engaging sound and clear visual performance throughout the home.

Surveillance

Protect your property with AI-assisted security cameras and instant mobile video access. Our surveillance systems support proactive monitoring and make it easy to check on your home at any moment.

Wi-Fi

Maintain fast, uninterrupted connectivity throughout your property with enterprise-grade networking. Our network infrastructure supports high-bandwidth streaming and multi-room AV while keeping connected home systems responsive.

Home Theater

Build a custom private cinema with high-performance sound and high-definition visual displays. We design dedicated media rooms that bring cinematic audio and video into a space built around how you enjoy movies at home.

Electrical Services

Complete electrical wiring supports safe, dependable power throughout your home. We provide backup generator installation along with low-voltage wiring, helping prepare your residence for future technology additions.
