Our Approach

Smart home design, coordinated from the first drawing.

The decisions that make a home feel effortless are made long before move-in — often before the walls exist. Most are never even raised.

From Consultation to Ongoing Support

What the smart home design and installation process includes.

Each project has its own construction schedule and technical scope, but the work follows a clear sequence so design decisions, field work, programming, and support stay connected.

01

Discovery

Walk the home or review the plans, define priorities, document constraints, and identify the routines the system should support.

02

System Design

Coordinate lighting, shades, audio, video, security, networking, power, controls, equipment locations, and future service access.

03

Infrastructure

Install and label cabling, conduit, shade power, network backhaul, rack infrastructure, and other work that belongs behind the finished surfaces.

04

Installation

Mount equipment and devices, terminate and test connections, coordinate trim details, and protect the architecture throughout the work.

05

Programming & Calibration

Program scenes and interfaces, tune networks, balance audio, calibrate video, set shade limits, and test the complete system room by room.

06

Handoff & Support

Train the household, deliver system information, monitor and service the installation, and adjust programming as the home evolves.

The Case for an Expert

Anyone can buy the technology. Far fewer can make it disappear into a home.

You have spent a lifetime earning this. You studied the land, interviewed the finest builders, and sat with designers who understood your taste. This is the home that will hold your family’s comfort and security for a generation.

It would be a quiet shame, then, to leave the systems you touch every day — the light, the climate, the sound, the security, the arrival and the rest — to off-the-shelf devices and a search bar. The hardware is a commodity. The intelligence that makes a home feel inevitable is not.

A seasoned integrator knows which questions to ask before the architect’s drawings are even final — the ones that keep the build on schedule and on budget, and deliver the security, ease, and quiet beauty you imagined from the very first conversation. That foresight is the difference between technology that competes with the home and technology that completes it.

Who We Work With

One technology partner for everyone at the table.

Architect, builder, interior designer, or homeowner — planning the technology early protects what each of you cares about most. Here is what working with TruDefinition looks like from where you sit.

Architects

Technology coordination that protects your design intent from the beginning.

  • Coordinate lighting, audio, video, shading, networking, power, and control
  • Plan infrastructure before construction details become difficult to change
  • Reduce visible clutter, awkward device placement, and late-stage revisions

Custom Builders

Clear planning, cleaner execution, and fewer avoidable surprises in the field.

  • Define low-voltage pathways, equipment locations, and service areas early
  • Coordinate rack locations, ventilation, power, network, and device placement
