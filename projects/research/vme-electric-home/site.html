About us

VME Electrical Contractor Inc. is a trusted licensed and insured electrical company based in East Hampton, NY, providing electrical services from Montauk to Southampton. With years of experience in the industry, we are dedicated to providing exceptional electrical services for both residential and commercial properties.

Our team of highly skilled electricians are committed to delivering high-quality workmanship and ensuring the utmost satisfaction of our customers. From electrical installations, repairs, maintenance and upgrades, we strive to meet the unique needs of each project efficiently and effectively. When you choose VME Electrical Contractor Inc., you can trust that your electrical needs are in reliable hands.
