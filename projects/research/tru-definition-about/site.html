A short story about homes that deserved better.

Dallas–Fort Worth Smart Home Design. Built Around How You Live.

Out of Frustration.

Built Around a Better Question.

TruDefinition started, like most good things, out of frustration. We kept walking into beautiful homes with forgettable technology. Six figures of equipment racked behind a closet door, gathering dust. Remote controls stacked on nightstands because nobody could remember which one did what. Owners who’d been sold a “smart home” and ended up with a sulking one.

So we built something different. Not another integrator. Not another firm racing to cram the most gear into the smallest closet. A company built around a question most companies never bother to ask:

How do you actually want to live in your home?

It hasn’t been designed yet.

If It Can’t Be Done, It Hasn’t Been Designed Yet.

This is how we approach each project: understand the home, document the constraints, and coordinate the technology before installation begins. Complex rooms require a better design conversation, not a larger pile of equipment.

The speaker locations conflict with the beam layout? We redesign the audio plan so the homeowner never knows there was a constraint. The floor plan leaves no room for a rack in the living space? We pull the equipment back to a single central closet, out of the rooms you live in, and run everything to it. We find a way. Every time.

Forty-plus years of combined experience informs the questions we ask about wiring, acoustics, lighting, networks, service access, and long-term support. See how that thinking carries through our smart home design and installation process.

The House Rules.

No catalog. No product list. No standard package. We start with you.

01

We Listen.

Before we sketch a single wire run, we sit with you. We learn how you wake. How you host. What annoys you about the place you’re in now. What you keep imagining for the place you’re moving into. Your life is the brief — not a product catalog.

02

We Design.

Shoulder-to-shoulder with your architect, builder, and interior designer from the very first plans. Technology is woven in, not bolted on. Speakers disappear into architecture. Keypads land where the eye expects them. Nothing is figured out later.

03

We Build.

Every cable, every connection, every line of code is placed with intent. The rack is a quiet work of engineering nobody will ever see — which is the point. The technology vanishes. The evening remains.

04

We Stay.

The relationship doesn’t end at the final walkthrough. When you call, you get someone who already knows your system, your house, and your preferences. Not a ticket number. A partner — for the life of the home.
