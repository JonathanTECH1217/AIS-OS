ABOUT

Since 1998, Holm Electric has partnered with homeowners and contractors, public works and improvement districts to offer professional residential and commercial electrical contracting, home automation and integration services. Our success can be attributed to a history of commitment to our core values of reliability, professionalism and efficiency.

When you can choose any Electrical, Audio & Video, Networking, Integration, or alarm specialists, to build your space you may find yourself asking…

WHY CHOOSE HOLM ELECTRIC FOR MY PROJECT?

The answer is in our approach to your project from start to finish and beyond. Holm Electric specializes in all the aforementioned areas and more, allowing us to offer a complete, comprehensive and custom-built solution for your space.

CUSTOM DESIGN A SOLUTION
Our team of Engineers and specialists will custom design a solution to meet your specific needs. From a simple lighting system to a fully integrated custom smart home or office, we have the knowledge expertise and experience to make even the most imaginative projects reality.

When it comes to your in-home theater system, smart home or office, E.V. charging station, high-speed network, or any other custom application, Holm Electric is here to deliver superior results every time.

We know every project has its own unique set of specifications and requirements, at Holm Electric we have the knowledge, experience and special expertise needed to ensure your space meets your expectations.

We approach your project by engineering and designing your system to fit your lifestyle today and in the future. Our knowledge of construction process and understanding of how all the pieces of the project will fit together give us a unique edge in creating the perfect installation for you.
HASSLE FREE
At Holm Electric we make engineering your project hassle free, whatever your needs might be, we eliminate the guesswork and frustration associated with many aspects of building, designing and successfully implementing smart solutions, networks, and electrical etc. From start to finish our team will work hard to ensure that your project functions as expected. By taking our systematic approach to designing and engineering every project we can ensure perfect results every time.
DOCUMENTED PROJECT
Upon completion you will have a fully and thoroughly documented project. If and when the time comes to service or upgrade your project you will have easily referenceable materials with supporting documentation and drawings, rather than have to depend on some installer’s memory who often can’t be found.
