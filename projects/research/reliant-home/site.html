Our Process

Scope & Discovery

Every project starts with a thorough evaluation of your home, electrical system, and goals. We review existing infrastructure, electrical load capacity, and any future upgrades you may be considering — from EV chargers and generators to full smart home automation.
This step ensures we design a solution that fits your home now and won’t limit you later.

Design & Estimate

Once the scope is defined, we design a system tailored to your home, lifestyle, and budget. Our estimates are clear, detailed, and transparent — no guesswork, no surprises.
We consider safety, performance, code compliance, and future expandability so your electrical system is built correctly from the start.

Build & Commission

Our licensed electricians and automation specialists execute the project with precision and professionalism. We follow best-practice installation standards, pull permits when required, and coordinate inspections to ensure everything meets or exceeds code.
Once installed, every system is tested, commissioned, and verified for proper operation before we consider the job complete.

Training & Handover

Technology should be easy to use. After installation, we walk you through your new electrical or smart home systems step by step. You’ll understand how everything works, how to control it, and how to get the most value from your investment.
No rushed explanations — we make sure you’re comfortable and confident before we leave.

Monitoring & Support

Our relationship doesn’t end at installation. We’re here to support your system long after the project is complete. From troubleshooting and maintenance to future upgrades, our team remains available to keep everything running smoothly.
As your home evolves, your electrical and automation systems should evolve with it.

Smart Home Evolution

Homes aren’t static — your electrical system shouldn’t be either. Through the Reliant SmartHome Evolution Plan, we help homeowners plan, phase, and upgrade their electrical and smart home systems over time.
Whether you’re starting with a panel upgrade today or planning for solar, battery backup, automation, or whole-home integration down the road, we design your system with a long-term roadmap in mind.
This approach avoids costly rework, protects your investment, and ensures your home is always ready for what’s next.
