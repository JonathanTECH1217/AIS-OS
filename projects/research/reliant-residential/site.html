Residential Services

At Reliant Electrical & Automation Inc., we’re proud to power homes throughout Fresno, Clovis, and Madera with safe, modern, and expertly integrated electrical solutions. Whether you’re building new, remodeling, or upgrading your home’s comfort and resilience, our licensed team brings experience, innovation, and a customer-first approach to every project.

Electrical Wiring & Panel Upgrades

From full-house rewiring to upgraded service panels, we ensure your home’s electrical infrastructure is safe, efficient, and future-ready. Whether you’re experiencing flickering lights or planning for solar and EV integration, we’ve got your power needs covered.

Home Automation

Simplify your everyday routines with intelligent automation. We design and install smart lighting, thermostats, motorized shades, security systems, and voice-controlled environments—all tailored to how you live. Control your entire home from your phone, even when you’re away.

Distributed Audio/Video

Fill every room with high-quality sound and seamless video access. Our whole-home audio/video systems bring entertainment and convenience together, with clean installations and intuitive controls for the entire family.

Home Theater Design

Turn movie nights into immersive experiences. We design, wire, and install custom home theaters that balance performance, acoustics, and aesthetics—bringing the cinema home with surround sound, projection, and integrated lighting.

Backup Power & Energy Resiliency

Prepare for outages and reduce reliance on the grid with generators, transfer switches, and energy storage systems. We design resilient backup power setups to keep your home running smoothly—even when the power goes out.

Permanent Holiday Lighting

Light up the season year-round with custom permanent holiday lighting. Our professional installations are discreet, weatherproof, and app-controlled—offering effortless celebration without tangled cords or seasonal ladders.

Electrical Inspections & Safety

Buying, selling, or renovating? We provide thorough electrical inspections to identify safety issues, outdated wiring, or code violations. We’ll help you make informed decisions and ensure your home is protected.

Ready to elevate your home?

Contact Reliant Electrical & Automation Inc. to schedule a free consultation or estimate. We’ll help you plan, power, and personalize your perfect living space.
