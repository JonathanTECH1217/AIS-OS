Our Roots!

At Reliant Electrical and Automation Inc., we’re more than just electricians — we’re integrators of innovation. Born from a foundation in industrial, maritime, entertainment and IT systems, our company has evolved into a modern electrical partner for clients who demand performance, intelligence, and future-readiness in every project.

From tugboats to smart homes, factory floors to festival grounds, we bring power, control, and creativity together in one seamless service. Our About Us section tells the story of this journey.

Our story began on the docks and in the industrial corridors — wiring vessels, providing sound reinforcement, and powering essential infrastructure. With a background in harsh-environment systems, high-voltage power, and mission-critical redundancy, we know what it means to build electrical work that lasts.

That same rugged, reliable mindset now fuels our work in residential, commercial, and creative spaces, where precision and protection meet artistry and experience.

We specialize in the intersection of energy, automation, and experience:

IT & Network Integration

Seamless low-voltage infrastructure for smart systems, AV, controls, and cloud-based energy management.

Sustainability-Driven Electrical

EV chargers, battery backup, load balancing, and energy-efficient lighting. We design with the future in mind.

Entertainment & Immersive Systems

Distributed audio/video, lighting control, and permanent festival infrastructure — from theaters to theme camps.

Automation & Smart Control

Custom control panels, PLC integration, home automation, and industrial sensors — whether it’s a show, a shop, or a ship.

Our Philosophy

We believe the best electrical systems are invisible but intelligent — reliable in function, efficient in operation, and intuitive for the people who use them. Every wire we pull and line we terminate serves a bigger goal: empowering our clients to work smarter, live better, and operate sustainably.
