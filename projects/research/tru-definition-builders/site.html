Builders, Architects, Designers — We See You.

Technology integration that respects your schedule, your finishes, and your name on the project.

A Trade That Behaves Like One.

You know the drill. The "AV guy" shows up halfway through trim, asks where the soffit went, and leaves a hole in your ceiling that wasn't on any plan. That has been getting it wrong for, well, way too long. No more.

We partner with custom home builders, remodelers, and architects across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex — early, quietly, and on your schedule. We've worked alongside some of DFW's most respected builders on projects from $500,000 starter homes to $10M+ estates, and the same standard walks through every door. Our job is to be the easiest trade on your project. Not the loudest.

Why Builders Keep Calling Us Back.

Early. On Purpose.

We come in during design — before framing — to walk pathways, equipment rooms, speaker locations, and power requirements. The result is fewer change orders, no awkward "we forgot the conduit" calls, and technology that disappears into the architecture instead of bolting on at the end.

Quiet Coordination.

We work directly with your project managers, electricians, and subs. We follow your schedule, show up when we said we would, and communicate before you have to ask. The trades that make builds easier get invited back. We try to be that.

The Walkthrough Moment.

When the homeowner walks in and everything works — lights, audio, security, shades, theater — it reflects on everyone in the chain. We exist to make that moment happen. Your name on the build, your client beaming, the technology behaving like it was always there.

For Interior Designers.

Technology should complete your design, not compete with it. We specialize in the kind that disappears: lighting systems that flatter your palette and textures, motorized shades that preserve sight lines, speakers tucked into ceilings, displays that vanish into millwork until someone reaches for the remote.

We coordinate closely on keypad finishes, speaker grille colors, shade fabrics, and equipment room locations that don't eat into usable square footage. Your aesthetic vision drives our technical decisions. Not the other way around.
