Do I need a permit for electrical work in Fresno, CA?

Yes. Most electrical work in Fresno — including panel upgrades, new circuits, EV chargers, generators, and major repairs — requires permits and inspections. Reliant Electrical & Automation handles the permitting process and coordinates inspections to ensure all work meets local and state electrical codes.

How do I know if my Fresno home needs an electrical panel upgrade?

Many Fresno homes were built before today’s electrical demands were common. Signs that a panel upgrade may be needed include frequent breaker trips, limited breaker space, flickering lights, or plans to add EV chargers, air conditioning upgrades, solar, or backup power. We perform load calculations to determine whether your existing panel is properly sized.

Are electrical panel upgrades common in older Fresno neighborhoods?

Yes. Older Fresno neighborhoods often have electrical systems that were not designed for modern appliances and equipment. Panel upgrades are one of the most common electrical improvements we perform, especially in homes built before the 1990s.

Can you install EV chargers in Fresno homes with older electrical systems?

Yes. Many Fresno homes can support EV charging with the right electrical upgrades. We assess your panel capacity, make any necessary improvements, and install EV charger circuits safely and in compliance with local codes.

Do power outages happen often enough in Fresno to justify a generator?

Power outages can occur in Fresno due to extreme heat, grid demand, or maintenance events. Backup generators and battery-ready electrical systems provide peace of mind by keeping essential circuits powered when outages occur.

Do you work with Fresno inspections and utility requirements?

Yes. We are familiar with Fresno inspection requirements and coordinate with local authorities as part of every permitted project. Our work is designed to pass inspection without delays or rework.

Do you only handle large projects, or do you also do smaller electrical repairs?

We handle both. From troubleshooting outlets and lighting issues to full electrical upgrades and automation planning, we provide professional service regardless of project size.

Do you serve all areas of Fresno?

Yes. We provide electrical services throughout Fresno and surrounding Central California communities.

How soon can electrical work be scheduled in Fresno?

Scheduling depends on project scope and permit requirements. Smaller repairs can often be scheduled quickly, while larger upgrades may require permitting and coordination. We provide clear timelines during the estimate process.
