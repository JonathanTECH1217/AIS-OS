## How act one was made on a page with no headline

Their first screen has no headline, no paragraph, and no button: it is an animated house map with a menu above it. The edit engine changes a headline and a button that already exist, and the page's own read takes the "Residential" menu link as the headline. So:

- The **headline** is their Residential menu link, moved into the hero at the render's spot and size. It leaves the menu as it lands.
- The **button** is their Contact menu link, moved under the headline and given the render's blue, size, and corners. It leaves the menu as it lands, and reads Contact (their own word) until he says "book a meeting".
- The **eyebrow** is a new line, placed at the render's spot.
- **Rule 11 check:** the last live frame and the first render frame match: the same headline glyphs, line breaks, and width (330 to 1110 px), the eyebrow at 215 against 214, the button at the same spot and size. What changes at the swap is the header bar (their menu becomes the phone and the button) and the paragraph under the headline, which he never names.
