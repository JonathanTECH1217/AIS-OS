## Live-edit notes (rule 11)

- Their page is Wix. The hero's photo is a Wix image that reloads its own picture whenever its box changes size, so the band's heights are pinned first, then the photo is swapped, then the picture is stretched to 680 px and the next band slides down 200 px. Their 75% photo fade is lifted; the scrim is a brightness step.
- The headline gets Newsreader with its optical size, the render's own cut, so the light serif matches stroke for stroke. The eyebrow gets Poppins 600 at 14 px with the render's letter spacing.
- Frame 20 (the last live frame) and frame 21 (the render) put the eyebrow, headline, copy, and button at the same sizes and heights: eyebrow at 222 px, headline at 268, button at 576. What changes at the swap is the header bar (theirs black and 100 px, the render's white and 80 px with the logo and phone): the page itself.
