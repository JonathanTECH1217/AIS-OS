## How the live edits land at the render's size (rule 11)

- Their page has no button, no eyebrow, and no copy in the hero, and the engine's `cta` and `copy` steps blank their own words when the element they aim at has no words in it. So the button is the carousel's hidden "Slide 1 of 11" line, made visible and styled; the copy is their footer's first line, rewritten and held at the render's spot (`position: fixed` at 445 px) over a hidden spacer of the copy's height.
- The carousel is hidden and the hero is held at the render's 700 px, its words centered in a 96 px padded box, so the eyebrow, headline, copy, and button land within a pixel of the render (221, 267, 444, 575 against 222, 268, 445, 576).
- The engine loads Newsreader without its optical-size axis, so the live headline ran 30 px narrower than the render's. It is set at 74.5 px (the render's is 72) to break and fill the same width.
- The header goes to 80 px with the logo on the left, the render's height. What still changes at the swap is the header itself: dark with their triangle logo becomes white with the wordmark and the phone. That is the page changing, not a size.
