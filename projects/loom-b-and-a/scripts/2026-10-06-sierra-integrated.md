## How the live edits were made

- Their chat box (LiveHelpNow) opens by itself partway through and covered the page; it is hidden from the first frame. Their "Let's talk!" tab goes with it.
- "Center" on their page builder (UIkit) needed help: their words sit in a half-width column, so the column was widened and stacked as one centered column, the button row centered, at the same second.
- The headline, eyebrow, line, and button land within 4 px of the render's heights. What changes at the swap: the top bar (theirs dark, the render's white), the photo's frame, and the button's words.

## Left for you

The render's brief read it as the hero, but the render's hero is 700 px tall, taller than theirs (540 px at 1440 by 900), so a 20 percent cut there would move away from the render.
