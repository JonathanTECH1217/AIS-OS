## How their page was edited

Their home page is a Divi slider, so the engine read "Welcome to Hatch Electric." under the slider as the headline, found no button, and the slider turns every seven seconds. The plan goes around it, with no change to the engine:

- **The slider is held** on the bedroom slide, the one on his screen: every slide gets the bedroom photo and the bedroom caption from the first frame (plan steps at 0:00). Nothing changes on screen; the page's own first shot had already turned to the lobby slide, so it is not used.
- **The hero's words are their own welcome block**, lifted over the photo at beat 5 and centered on the render's middle line, so the headline, eyebrow, and copy edits land where the render puts them. The old caption box goes at the same beat.
- **The button is their "Learn more." link** from the Home Controls card (below the first screen), restyled into the render's button and set at the render's spot. A new button made from nothing comes out with no words (see Left for you).
- **Rule 11 check:** state 36, the last live change, against state 37, the render: eyebrow top 222, headline top 268 and 151 tall on two lines, copy 640 wide on three lines, button 241 by 64 at 576. The same on both. The button's words may show in a fallback face if Manrope is slow to load on their page. What changes at the swap is the header (their menu becomes the phone and a gold button), which is the page itself changing.

## Left for you

- **Two cuts of yours stay out:** "in California. If you're in the San Francisco Bay Area... San Francisco or Oakland" (they are in South Lake Tahoe) and "relative to the trade per landing page". Say if either goes back in.
- **The gold has no beat of its own.** At "this gold color is the primary" nothing moves; the gold shows on the eyebrow and the button when you name them.
- **The four form steps run under a second apart**, because you count them fast. They read as a flick through the form.
- **Their reviews and the about photo are empty boxes on the render**, as in its brief: their site shows no reviews and no owner photo. The video shows the boxes when you name them.
- **Engine, not fixed here:** a `cta` change on a page with no button makes a button with no words (`setText` blanks the text it just wrote), and the headline read takes the largest words in the first screen even when they sit under a slider. Both went around in the plan.
