# The three video scripts

Drafted 2026-10-03 from the interview (`brainstorms/2026-10-03-meta-ads-builder-search-check.md`, Q1 to Q4, Q13, Q14). Not approved until Jonathan says so; nothing is recorded before that.

Rules every script follows (`references/cold-call-principles.md`, `references/voice.md`, the Style Guide): every line lowers threat or raises curiosity; honesty is the tactic; specific beats smooth; their outcome, never our service; one small ask. Under 30 seconds read aloud (Ad Multiplier's limit, and Meta's attention). Sixth-grade words. No hedge. No name. No price. No dollar figure. No tenure. No "no setup fee". No PHA. AIA is allowed. The hook is on screen as text before he speaks (hooks brainstorm, format notes). The same ASK and LOCK on all three, so the read compares hooks, not asks.

Line jobs: HOOK, BOUND, FILTER, PROVE, NAME, ASK, LOCK, HOLD.

## The three objections under the ASK, and where each is answered

The principles file requires three written objections per ASK line. In a video nobody answers back, so each is answered on the page or on the call.

1. **"All my work is referrals. I don't need search."** The result line for "Referrals" (`check.md`): the builder who searched hired someone else. On the call: the hook 16 logic, then the county's search results on the screen.
2. **"I tried ads once. They didn't work."** The result line for "We did once": the call reads what the ads bought and what the page did with the click. On the call: the search results and the page they landed on, side by side.
3. **"I don't have time for a call."** The ad itself: thirty seconds, then fifteen minutes, afternoons, two weeks out, "Bring nothing" in the confirmation. Nothing is asked that takes longer than that.

A fourth, unspoken: **"Who are you?"** No name is said. The Page is Monarc Build, the face is his, the proof is one job, the credential is the AIA talks, and the About line on the page is "Sold and installed before we marketed."

## Recording notes

- 4K, vertical (9:16), 30 fps, the phone or camera at eye level, the lens where his eyes land.
- A good microphone (a lavalier or a shotgun), 48 kHz; the raw audio is the audio of the finished ad.
- Any room. Even light on the face, no hard shadow across it, so the clothing and background swap has a clean edge to work with.
- Three takes per script. Two seconds of silence at the head and the tail. Each take 30.0 seconds or under.
- Hands still or below the frame; a big arm move across the body fights the clothing swap. Brim up so the eyes show. No logo anywhere in frame (the PHA polo stays in the closet).
- Say the hook straight at the lens; the on-screen text covers the first second anyway.
- Files into `media/inbox/`, named `meta-01-belief-t1.mp4`, `meta-01-belief-t2.mp4`, and so on through `meta-03-referrals-t3.mp4`. Studio prepares each one (MP4 copy, waveform, transcript); the transcript seeds the captions.
- Pick one take per script. Then the Higgsfield pass (`README.md`, Production), then the cut in Studio.
