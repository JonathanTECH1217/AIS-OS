## e. What AEO searchers type

Owners type the outcome, not the acronym: "how to get my business to show up in chatgpt" (Thrive, HubSpot, Sagapixel, PR Newswire, Quora all title it that way). Agencies split the name six ways: answer engine optimization, generative engine optimization or GEO, AI search optimization, AI SEO, AI visibility, ChatGPT SEO, LLM SEO (plumberseo.net lists four on one page: https://www.plumberseo.net/ai-search-optimization/). Trade agencies target "ai search optimization for contractors" and "ai visibility for contractors". Paid ads on these terms: could not verify; the tools show organic only. Jonathan's SERP screenshots (method step 5) settle it. One published price: Hook AEO $4,000/mo.

## f. h1 candidates

1. "Local SEO for Contractors": mirrors two buy terms; "local" filters national SEO shoppers. Blue Corona owns the exact page.
2. "SEO for Home Service Companies": RYNO's frame; covers all five trades in one term; no trade hook.
3. "Google Business Profile Management for Contractors": the map listing is the pain in every quote; only small agencies rank it; narrows the page to one of its four parts.

## g. Risks

1. Head-term drift. Every trade plus "seo company" or "seo services" has Thrive, Blue Corona, or RYNO national pages; expect $20 to $60 clicks. Exact and phrase only, trade word first.
2. AEO vocabulary is unsettled and B2B-skewed; "geo" is ambiguous. Run as a test, not a pillar.
3. GBP terms pull Fiverr and "GMB" searchers wanting a $50 fix, and Google's own help page ranks. Qualify with "for contractors" and anchor a price on the page.
