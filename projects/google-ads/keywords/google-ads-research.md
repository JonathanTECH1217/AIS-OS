## f. Risks

1. Head terms are owned: "google ads management", "home services ppc agency", "hvac ppc agency" sit with Blue Corona, Scorpion, RYNO, Hook, Third Marble. Never bid them.
2. Homeowner and product traps: Google Home/Nest on seed 1; Sona's bid lists are homeowner terms; "lighting control leads" is cables; "lutron dealer leads" reads as lead times.
3. Low volume: no page ranks for "ppc for smart home installers"; brand-adjacent dealer terms return only vendor pages. Expect the integrator terms under 10 a month; the other trades carry volume and the most agencies.

Not verified: forum bodies (ContractorTalk, HVAC-Talk, PlumbingZone, ElectricianTalk are paywalled; Mike Holt 403; Reddit, Quora, archive.org blocked); Built Right pages (titles from SERP only); the search budget ran out before PAA-style queries for roofing and plumbing.
