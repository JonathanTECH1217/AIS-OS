## e. Naming verdict

"Facebook ads" leads, Instagram second, "Meta" a test, "social media" parked.

- Owners: 19 forum threads from "meta ads" and "instagram ads" searches; none titled Meta or Instagram. A 2025 thread is titled "Facebook ads".
- Nine seeds, 72 unique pages: Facebook 44, social media 11, Meta 7 (all agencies), Instagram 4.
- Bias checks: LeadsBridge's Facebook page tops "meta ads for contractors"; 2 of 9 Instagram-seed titles say Instagram; 5 of 6 "social media" titles mean marketing.
- Agencies title Facebook, name the product Meta (Hook, CMP, Contractor Dynamics).
