## e. Software shoppers versus done-for-you shoppers

Test, top nine results: product pages, tool lists and monthly prices mean software; builders, paid staff and setup fees mean done for you. Three builders: done-for-you possible; seven software pages: software-owned.

- Done-for-you possible: back office (4 builders), lead follow up (3), ai automation for contractors (3).
- Software-owned: invoicing, bookkeeping, missed call, receptionist, both answering seeds.
- Wrong reader: proposals, ai for small contractors.

The CSV keeps "missed call text back for contractors" a candidate; the test says software. The buyer exists: 53% of 1,000+ contractors call complexity their biggest AI worry (https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166074-survey-25-of-residential-contractors-using-ai-to-boost-business).

## g. Risks

1. **"ai contractor"** is a freelance AI engineer or job (Arc.dev, Upwork, https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Ai-Contractor) and a product name.
2. **"automation contractor"** is factory controls: Rockwell's "Main Automation Contractor" (https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/capabilities/consulting-integration-services/main-automation-contractor.html). It bleeds into "automation integrator". Add industrial, plc, scada.
3. **Price anchor.** Software shows $18 to $449 a month (https://www.withallo.com/blog/best-ai-receptionists-for-contractors); builders start at $2,500. Integrators' own tools (D-Tools, Crestron, Savant) showed AI quote builders at CEDIA 2026 (https://www.cepro.com/news/ai-on-the-cedia-expo-floor-what-the-smart-home-industry-is-actually-building/628680).
