Date 2026-09-25. Landing page `/website-build/`. Volumes, CPCs and ad counts stay blank until Jonathan's Planner run and SERP read. Reddit blocks this tool, the ContractorTalk threads sit behind a bot paywall (HTTP 402), and Mike Holt's forum returned 403, so the pain quotes below are thinner than asked and each is labeled.

## a. Who ranks per seed

- **website design for contractors**: Contractor Gorilla ("Website Design Services for Contractors"), Sites4Contractors, Awebco, ContractorWeb, next to Webflow and Hook example lists. Both "website design for contractors" and "contractor website design" are used.
- **contractor website design company**: all agencies (Freshy, Thrive, DreamCo, Webrunner, Cybernautic). Titles use "company" and "agency" about equally.
- **website for electrical contractors**: SaaS listicles (Jobber, FieldEdge, ServiceTitan), Wix, MayeCreate. Agencies only appear on "electrician website design company" (Blue Corona, Web Design Mechanic, Relentless).
- **home automation website design**: templates only (Webflow, ThemeForest, TemplateMonster, MotoCMS, Dribbble). No agency on page one.
- **hvac website design company**: Blue Corona, HVAC Webmasters, HVAC Web Works, Relentless, Hook. Cleanest agency page of the set.
- **roofing website design**: Dribbble, Webflow, ThemeForest, Pinterest beside RoofWebs, Relentless, Hook. Adding "company" clears the templates (Thrive, Split Reef, Roofing Webmasters).
- **plumbing website design**: Simpro, Webflow, Wix, Dribbble, Pinterest; agencies are Relentless and Plumbing Webmasters. "plumber website design company" is all agencies (Thrive, Blue Corona, Solvis, Strictly Plumbers).
- **website that generates leads for contractors**: Google reads it as lead marketplaces (Angi, Houzz, ServiceTitan's list). Not bid as typed.
- **redesign my contractor website**: Hook tips, Sixth City, Tenaya360, Page Progressive, OTLM. Mixed learn and buy.
- **av company website design**: "AV" means Antelope Valley here; AV Web Designs (California) owns the page, One Firefly is sixth. "av integrator website design" fixes it (One Firefly, Specifi, ServiceBuilds HQ).
- **landing pages for contractors**: tools (Landingi, Instapage, TemplateMonster) mixed with Webrunner, Reno Rise, Contracting Empire.

## e. H1 target candidates

1. **website design for contractors** (4 words). Widest seed, agency SERP, covers all five trades on one page. Risk: shares the page with example lists and the $12,000 to $24,000 players.
2. **contractor website design company** (4 words). All-agency SERP, "company" is a buy signal, mirrors DreamCo and Webrunner titles. Stiffer in a headline.
3. **av integrator website design** (4 words). One Firefly is the only entrenched name, and it is the trade Jonathan comes from. Risk: volume may be under 10 a month.

## f. Risks

1. Head-term bleed: phrase match on "website design company" and "web design" pulls the national head terms; Hook, Blue Corona, Scorpion, RYNO sit on every trade variant.
2. Template bleed: roofing, plumbing and home automation "website design" pages are half templates and Dribbble, so the negative list must ship at launch and "home automation website design" stays parked.
3. AV ambiguity: "av company" means Antelope Valley; the clean integrator terms are thin and may fall under the 10-a-month floor.
