# Kickoff emails

Two drafts the `/kickoff` skill fills from the capture. Jonathan sends; the AIOS never sends. Rules from `references/voice.md`: open with the first name and the point, under 120 words, terms stated flat, no hedges, no em dashes, sign-off "Jonathan" (this is not first touch).

## Before the call: the invite

**Subject:** Kickoff {Day, date} at {time}, 45 minutes

{First name},

Kickoff is {Day, date} at {time} {tz}, 45 minutes, Google Meet: {Meet link}

Have these open when we start: your Google Business Profile, Google Ads if you have it, and whoever holds the domain login. We add me as a manager on the call so the build starts the same day.

Bring the three projects you are proudest of, with photos.

Jonathan

## After the call: the recap

**Subject:** Kickoff recap and what I need by {date}

{First name},

Captured today: {top three services}, {city list, short}, floor of ${floor}, booked into {calendar owner}'s calendar.

Build goes live {build deadline}.

Still need from you:
{pending access line 1: tool, exactly how to grant it}
{pending access line 2}
{photos or files line}

Weekly report lands {weekday} at {recipient}. Monthly 15 minutes: {weekday, time}.

{VSL line, only if Block 7 did not run: "Below is the video brief. Eleven short answers, your words. Back to me by {date} and I'll have the script to you two days later."}

Jonathan

## Blanks

- {Meet link}: from the calendar event the AIOS creates.
- {floor}, {calendar owner}, {build deadline}: from `projects/clients/{slug}/brief.md`.
- Pending lines: every row in `access.md` still `requested`, one line each, with the grant path from `access-checklist.md`.
- {VSL line}: include only when the call skipped Block 7. Paste the "Fill-in brief" from `templates/vsl-draft.md` under the signature, draft lines filled from the capture.
- Never add a price, term, or promise the client did not hear on the call.
