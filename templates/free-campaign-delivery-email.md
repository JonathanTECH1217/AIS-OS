# Free campaign for a review: the delivery email

Sent after the prospect said yes on the call (the second window, `references/free-campaign-call-script.md`). Drafted by `scripts/free_campaign.py deliver` into Proton Drafts with `audit.pdf` and `ads-editor.csv` attached; Jonathan sends. Written 2026-10-03 from `references/mycopy.md`: first name, the point, under 120 words, no hedges, no em dashes, no price unless asked, nothing repeated from the call. The review line is honest and has no strings. The last line is the retainer, said once.

Blanks the script fills: {first_name}, {company}, {city}, {service}, {budget}, {page}.
