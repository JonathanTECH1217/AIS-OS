# The Loom B and A email

The email that carries a Loom B and A video (`.claude/skills/loom-b-and-a/workflows/send-email.md`). Jonathan, 2026-10-06: "we'll test different subject lines and brief amounts of copy that get them to open the link and watch the video more frequently." AIOS draft, not approved.

Rules (`references/mycopy.md`): open with the first name and a comma; 35 to 90 words; no hedges, no em dashes, no bullets, no price; one signature phrase at most; "My Best, Jonathan" on a first touch. The video is the proof; the email only gets it watched. Never "prospect".

## On test now

Subject lines, all three in rotation, with body B1. The rotation is read from `projects/loom-b-and-a/sends.json` (the fewest sends goes next). When one subject line has about 20 sends and a lead, it stays and the bodies go on test.

## Subject lines

- **S1:** `Your {service} page, rebuilt in three minutes`
- **S2:** `{First name}, I rebuilt your first screen`
- **S3:** `{Company}: six changes, three minutes`

(S3's "six" is the count of changes in that piece; fill it from the plan.)

## Bodies

**B1**, about 65 words:

```
{First name},

I recorded three minutes on your {service} page: the first screen as it is, then each change as I make it. The headline, the button, the words, the picture.

{Loom link}

Nothing to send me. Watch it, keep what fits, and if you want the changes made, give me the word.

My Best,
Jonathan
```

**B2**, about 35 words:

```
{First name},

Your {service} page, before and after, three minutes, my voice over it:

{Loom link}

Keep what fits. If you want it built, give me the word.

My Best,
Jonathan
```

## Blanks

| Blank | From |
|---|---|
| `{First name}` | the company's file (the CRM, the call list's owner, the research folder) |
| `{service}` | the page's service, in the buyer's words ("lighting control") |
| `{Company}` | their name as they write it |
| `{Loom link}` | the share link of the cut once it is on Loom, or the link he gives |
| `three minutes` | the cut's real length, rounded; under three say "two minutes" |
