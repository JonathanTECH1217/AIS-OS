# Follow-up email after a booked cold call

The LOCK step of the cold call script (`references/cold-call-principles.md`). Send within minutes of the call, while the yes is warm. Written 2026-09-09. Edit here; the AIOS fills it from the call details on request ("book {name}, {email}, {day} {time}").

Rules: under 80 words, restate the exact time, one line of what they get, one line to make rescheduling easy, the site link, sign-off. No pitch. No em dashes.

## Email

**Subject:** {Day} at {time}, 15 minutes, bring nothing

{First name},

Good talking just now. Locking in what we said:

**{Day, date} at {time} Eastern, 15 minutes, Google Meet.** Link: {Meet link}

I'll bring what buyers in {metro} are searching for right now and where your site shows up for it today. You bring nothing.

If the time slips, reply with a better one and I'll move it.

What we do, on one page: monarcbuild.com

My Best,
Jonathan
{phone}

## Day-before reminder

**Subject:** Tomorrow at {time}

{First name}, we're on for tomorrow at {time} Eastern. Same link: {Meet link}. See you then.

My Best,
Jonathan

## Blanks

- {First name}
- {Day, date} and {time}: confirm the prospect's time zone on the call; write both if they're outside Eastern.
- {metro}: the city or county they serve, in their words.
- {Meet link}: from the calendar invite. The AIOS can create the invite (Google Calendar is connected).
- {phone}: not yet on file in the AIOS.
