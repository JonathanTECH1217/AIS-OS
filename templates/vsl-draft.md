# VSL script draft

The video in the hero of monarcbuild.com and of every landing page Monarc builds for a client (`projects/Landing Page Build/Section Spec.md`, Hero, Media; `Style Guide.md` rule 2, 16:9 slot). Adapts the cold call jobs (`references/cold-call-principles.md`) to video so the call, the email, and the video run on one system. Written 2026-09-13. Two uses: Monarc's own VSL (worked example below, Jonathan on camera) and each client's, filled from Block 7 of the kickoff questionnaire (`.claude/skills/kickoff/assets/questionnaire.md`). The AIOS drafts v1 from the brief; the speaker records; nothing is published without approval. Client scripts live at `projects/clients/{slug}/vsl.md`.

Rules: 60 to 90 seconds. Under 220 words. One viewer per video; a second audience gets a second video. One job per line. Their outcome, never the service. Say it is a sales video. No em dashes. No hedges. One artifact carries the proof on screen. Every ASK has three objections listed with the line that pre-empts each. Never name a competitor. Nothing on screen the speaker cannot show. For Monarc: never name PHA or show its logo (`context/about-business.md`), and no years or start date aloud or on screen (Section Spec, About, Q19).

## Jobs

Each line carries exactly one job. No job, cut it. Two jobs, split it. Read aloud under 80 seconds so the promise of 90 is kept with room.

| Job | What the line does | Seconds | Cold call parent |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | Name what the viewer typed or is thinking, in their words. First three seconds. No logo intro. | 0 to 5 | DISARM |
| BOUND | Say it is a sales video and price the cost of watching: the length, and what is at the end. | 5 to 10 | BOUND |
| FILTER | Who this is for and who it is not: area, project band, buyer type. The targeting, spoken aloud. | 10 to 20 | QUALIFY |
| PROVE | Insider credential in trade vocabulary. The artifact on screen carries the proof. | 20 to 35 | PROVE |
| NAME | Their problem in their words, ending on the question they already ask themselves. | 35 to 50 | NAME |
| ASK | Outcome, small ask, honest intent, one specific next step. | 50 to 70 | ASK |
| LOCK | What happens after they act. Spoken, and in writing on screen. | 70 to 80 | LOCK |
| HOLD | End card. Three seconds. No talking. The button label and the phone on screen. | 80 to 85 | SILENCE |

## Script skeleton

Bold label, what is on screen in parentheses, then the line. Fill from the brief. Keep each line to one or two sentences.

**HOOK** ({speaker}, tight, no title card)
{viewer} in {area}. You searched {term}. This is the answer.

**BOUND** ({speaker})
It is a sales video. {n} seconds, then a button.

**FILTER** (caption: {area}, {project band})
It is for {viewer}s doing {project band} in {area}. If that is not you, close it now.

**PROVE** ({artifact} full frame, {speaker} voice over, then back to {speaker})
I'm {speaker}. {credential}. {proof job}: what was in it, and the number.

**NAME** ({speaker})
{fear 1}. {fear 2}. {fear 3}. You want {outcome}. So who do you trust with it?

**ASK** ({speaker})
We do {outcome}. {next step}, and I will try to sell you {the offer in one phrase}.

**LOCK** (caption: {after}, in writing)
{after}.

**HOLD** (end card: button label from the page, {phone}, {email})
No audio. Three seconds.

## Recording checklist

- Under 90 seconds. Read the script aloud with a timer before setting up the camera.
- 16:9. Phone horizontal, chest up, eyes at lens height.
- No logo intro. The first three seconds are the HOOK line.
- Captions: burned in or an `.srt` alongside. Most viewers watch muted on a phone.
- The artifact is legible at phone width. One artifact, four to six seconds.
- Monarc photos: check the PHA exposures in `context/about-business.md` before anything goes public.
- Hosting: decided per client at build (their site or Monarc's). Monarc's own: pending Jonathan (Q7).
- Until the file lands, the page ships with the three hero stills and the "Video coming" caption (Style Guide rule 2). The layout does not change when the video arrives.

## Blanks

- {viewer}: Q34. One person. If the client names two, the second gets a second video.
- {term}: Q32, or the service x city term of the page the video sits on.
- {area}: Q3, the first two names on the ordered list, in the client's words.
- {project band}: Q4 floor, Q5 typical, Q14 new build vs retrofit. Spoken as a band, never as the floor alone.
- {outcome}: Q35, the client's words for what the viewer wants done. Never a service name.
- {fear 1}, {fear 2}, {fear 3}: Q36. Three. Fewer than three means ask again.
- {the offer in one phrase}: the pilot or the tier as stated on the page, in six words or fewer. Monarc: "a sixty day pilot." No price aloud.
- {slug}: the client folder name, lowercase hyphens, from the kickoff prep.
- {company}: the client's trading name on the fill-in brief. Monarc: "Monarc Build".
- {speaker}, {credential}: Q37, checked against Q7. Never a claim the speaker cannot show.
- {proof job}, {artifact}: Q38, one of the Q8 projects. Photo, walkthrough, or proposal page. Permission on file in the brief.
- {next step}, {after}: Q39. Level 2 clients: the 5 minute call, 9 to 7 Eastern, named person. Level 1: the number or the booking link, and who answers.
- {banned}, {required}: Q40. Competitor names are always banned.
- {logistics}: Q41. Recording deadline inside the 14 day build.
- {n}: the spoken length. Say the real number; 80 if the read is 70 to 78 seconds.
- {phone}, {email}: from the client brief. Monarc: jonathan@monarcbuild.com; phone not yet on file.
