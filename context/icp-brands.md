## How the AIOS uses this (notes, not part of Jonathan's list)

- High-end signal: an integrator that's a Crestron, Savant, Josh.ai, Lutron HomeWorks, Kaleidescape, or Steinway Lyngdorf dealer, or an HTSA or ProSource member, is more likely to be doing the $2M to $25M homes Monarc targets.
- List building: Crestron, Control4, Savant, Lutron, Josh.ai, Sonos, and Kaleidescape all publish public dealer locators. Searching those by service area is the fastest way to a qualified prospect list.
- Landing pages: one page per service maps to these categories (control and automation, lighting, shades, distributed audio, theater, video, networking, security, energy). Brand names on the page are the ones the client specs, from the kickoff questionnaire.
