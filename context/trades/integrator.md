## Services (one landing page category each)

Control and automation; lighting control; lighting fixtures and design; motorized shades; distributed audio, indoor and outdoor; home theater and media room; video distribution and displays; networking and wifi; security, surveillance, and access; power, racks, and surge; energy (backup power, solar, EV); prewire for new construction; service plans and remote support.
