## Warranty names a homeowner asks about

Carrier and Bryant 10 year parts with registration; Trane 10 or 12 year registered limited; Lennox 10 year parts, some lines 20 year compressor (confirm terms); Mitsubishi Electric 12 year parts and compressor with a Diamond Contractor install (confirm terms); Daikin 12 year parts with registration; WaterFurnace 10 year parts and labor options. Extended labor warranties are the dealer's own product (questionnaire item 11).

## How the AIOS uses this (notes)

- High-end signals: Diamond Elite plus a Factory Authorized Dealer badge together, geothermal, radiant and hydronics, wine cellar cooling, Manual J design for new construction, Viessmann or Buderus boilers.
- List building: Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, and WaterFurnace publish dealer locators searchable by zip.
- Landing pages: one page per service above. Brand names on the page are the ones the client ticked and starred in T2, with the program from T3 only when the certificate is on file.
- Kickoff Q7 for HVAC: look up the Carrier, Trane, Lennox, and Mitsubishi locators before the call and confirm.
- The license number prints on the site (asset email item 3); HVAC and mechanical licensing is state or county specific.
