## Services (one landing page category each)

Asphalt shingle roof replacement; designer shingles (Grand Sequoia, Presidential, Berkshire class); standing seam metal; metal shingle and stone-coated steel; natural slate; synthetic slate and shake; cedar shake and shingle; clay and concrete tile; copper bays, porches, and accents; flat and low-slope (TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen); roof repair and leak repair; storm damage and insurance claims; seamless and copper gutters; gutter guards; skylights and sun tunnels; attic ventilation and insulation; chimney flashing and custom sheet metal; siding, soffit, fascia; solar roofing; roof inspections (real estate, maintenance).

## Warranty names a homeowner asks about

GAF Golden Pledge, Silver Pledge, System Plus. Owens Corning Platinum Protection, Preferred Protection. CertainTeed SureStart PLUS (3-Star, 4-Star, 5-Star). DaVinci lifetime limited. Manufacturer warranties register through the contractor's program tier; the tier a client holds decides which names can print.

## How the AIOS uses this (notes)

- High-end signals: slate, copper, standing seam, DaVinci, Ludowici, cedar. A roofer holding Master Elite and Platinum Preferred together is doing volume with two majors.
- List building: GAF, Owens Corning, CertainTeed, DaVinci, and VELUX publish contractor locators searchable by zip. Searching those by service area is the fastest way to a qualified prospect list.
- Landing pages: one page per service above. Brand names on the page are the ones the client ticked and starred in T2, with the program from T3 only when the certificate is on file.
- Kickoff Q7 for roofers: look up the GAF and Owens Corning locators before the call and confirm.
- The license number prints on the site (asset email item 3); many state boards require it on every ad.
