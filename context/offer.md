## Monarc Build

**Booked appointments with high ticket prospects.**

For home integrators doing residential work in high profile homes.

### Level 1: $2,500/mo

Ad spend is paid on top. $1,000/mo minimum recommended.

- Website. New build, or we take over your existing site onto our hosting if you have the HTML.
- One service landing page per offering and per service area.
- Managed Google Ads running behind those pages.
- SEO: local SEO, Google Business Profile, content, technical.
- Weekly emailed PDF report. One 15 minute meeting a month.

### Level 2: $4,500/mo

Everything in Level 1, plus First Responder.

- A person, not a bot, calls every lead within 5 minutes of a form submission. 9am to 7pm Eastern, Monday through Friday. Email follow-up after the call. After-hours leads get the first call at 9am the next business morning.
- Qualifies against your pricing floor and service area, then books the appointment straight into your calendar.
- Month to month. Add it or drop it any time.

### 60 day pilot

- Start on Level 1 for 60 days. No setup fee.
- We match $1,000 in ad spend during the pilot.
- Day 60: continue on a 6 month term, or walk. If you walk, you own the site, the landing pages, and all reporting data.
