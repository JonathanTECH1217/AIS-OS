## Monarc Build

**Booked appointments with high ticket prospects.**

For home integrators and low voltage contractors doing residential and commercial work.

### The 60-day pilot: Google Ads management

Monarc will:

- Rebuild the Google Ads campaigns around high-intent keywords.
- Cut the lead form to 3 fields and place it above the fold on the landing page.
- Match landing page copy to the target searches.
- Tag every form fill and booking with the keyword and campaign it came from.
- Diagnose impression share and report whether budget or ad rank is the limit.
- Send a monthly report: spend, leads by source, cost per lead.

### Pilot fees

- Management fee: $2,000 per month.
- Ad spend: $2,000 per month, paid by the client directly to Google.
- Monarc match: $1,000 per month, applied as a credit against the management fee for both pilot months.
- The client pays $1,000 per month to Monarc and $2,000 per month to Google. Total $3,000 per month, $6,000 for the pilot.

### Term

60 days, starting the day the client completes access. The pilot is a fixed commitment; neither party may cancel it early.

### After the pilot

Month to month at $2,500 per month management plus a $2,000 per month minimum ad spend paid by the client to Google. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice.

### Onboarding fee

$3,000. Waived if the client signs on or before September 30, 2026.

### Access

Within 5 business days of signing, the client gives Monarc admin access to Google Ads, Google Analytics, the website and hosting, and the CRM or lead inbox. The pilot clock starts when access is complete.

### Client responsibilities

- Respond to every lead within one business day.
- Approve copy and landing pages within 2 business days of receipt.
- Attend one 15-minute check-in per week.
- Keep a valid payment method on the Google Ads account.

### Payment

Monarc invoices the management fee on the first day of each month, due within 7 days. Work pauses if payment is more than 10 days late. Google bills ad spend to the client directly.

### Ownership, guarantee, confidentiality, law

The client owns the Google Ads account, the ad data, the lead data, and the landing pages once all fees are paid. Monarc keeps its templates, process, and tooling. Lead volume and closed jobs depend on the market and on the client's follow-up; Monarc does not guarantee either. Each party keeps the other's business information private during and after the agreement. Governed by the laws of the State of Maryland.

### The math, as Jonathan says it on the call (Chisholm draft, 2026-09-15)

$2k in monthly ad spend at $125 a lead is 16 leads. At a $10k to $15k ticket and 30% gross margin, one install is $3k to $4.5k in gross profit. Close 1 of 16 and the month's marketing is covered. After the pilot it is $4.5k a month all in; two installs a month cover that. Judge it on what a job is worth over five years, not on cost per lead.

### Proof

We ranked an integrator #1 for "whole home audio in Annapolis." A contractor restoring a Georgian Colonial found that page: $70k of James Loudspeaker, whole home audio indoors and out, wired from rough-in. That one contractor is now over $500k in lifetime value. Separately, $2.5k of Google Ads pulled a $91k waterfront great room fitted with Lutron Ketra lighting, and a $50k Control4 and Lutron retrofit in a Baltimore penthouse.
