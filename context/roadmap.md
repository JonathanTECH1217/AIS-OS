# Roadmap: from here to $1M

Written 2026-09-07. Target: $1,000,000 in cash and investments in Jonathan's name, outside the agency. Path: 5 clients, then 15, then 30, then hold or sell. Honest timeline 3 to 5 years.

Every step below produces one thing and feeds one loop. Rates marked "assumed" get replaced by the tally after two weeks of dials.

## The five loops

| Loop | Runs | Trigger | The number | The decision it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dial | Daily, tally Friday | A dial | Dials, connects, booked, showed, closed | Which one script line gets rewritten this week |
| Lead | Every form fill | A lead comes in | Minutes to first call, qualified, booked, showed, sold | Is Level 2 worth $2k; does this client stay |
| Proof | Day 60 per client | Client hits day 60 | One result in dollars or rank | What goes on the offer page and in the call frame |
| Money | Monthly | Month end | Revenue, costs, salary, invested, profit | Every hire and every price change |
| Capacity | Monthly | Month end | Hours per client, your hours in delivery | Hire, raise price, or say no |

## Stage 0: this week. Build the plumbing.

1. Open a business bank account and a payment and invoicing tool. Output: revenue has a landing place. Feeds Money.
2. Build one tracking sheet with four tabs: Dials, Pipeline, Leads, Money. Output: nothing lives in your head. Feeds Dial, Lead, Money, Capacity.
3. Finish the offer doc: contact line, confirm the match amount. Output: something to send after every yes. Feeds Proof.
4. Run the script drill: one job per line, cut 20%, three objections under every ask. Output: script v1 with objections. Feeds Dial.
5. Write the follow-up email the script ends on. One screen, offer page attached, the meeting time restated. Output: the LOCK step exists. Feeds Dial.
6. Build a list of 200 integrators in your target areas: owner name, phone, site, GBP rank for one service term. Output: 9 weeks of dials at 22 a day. Feeds Dial.
7. Write the kickoff questionnaire script: services, brands per service, pricing floor, service area, calendar access. Output: First Responder inputs, same for every client. Feeds Lead.
8. Write two money rules and date them: your monthly salary, and the percent of profit invested. Output: the rules the Money loop enforces. Feeds Money.

## Stage 1: now to December 1. Five clients. You are the whole company.

Assumed rates: 30% connect, 10% of connects book, 50% show, 25% close. That's about 1,300 dials, 400 conversations, 40 booked, 20 held, 5 signed.

9. Dial 22 a day, first hour, Monday to Friday. Log every call: name, line where it died, outcome. Feeds Dial.
10. Friday: tally the line where calls died. Rewrite that one line only. Feeds Dial.
11. Every booked meeting: confirmation email same day, reminder the day before. Log show or no-show. Feeds Dial.
12. Run the 15 minute meeting from the offer page. Log close or the objection that killed it. Feeds Dial and Proof.
13. On a yes: invoice the same day, book the kickoff, run the questionnaire, get calendar access. Feeds Money and Lead.
14. Build: site or takeover, one landing page per service and per area, GBP, Ads live. Set a build deadline (suggest 14 days from kickoff) and log hours. Feeds Lead and Capacity.
15. Be the First Responder: every form fill called within 5 minutes, 9am to 7pm Eastern, email follow-up, booked into their calendar. Log every lead: time in, time called, qualified, booked, showed, sold. Feeds Lead.
16. Every client, every week: emailed PDF with leads, calls, booked, showed, sold, spend. Feeds Lead and Proof.
17. Month end: revenue, costs, pay your salary, invest the percent, hours per client. Fifteen minutes. Feeds Money and Capacity.

## Stage 2: December 2026 to June 2027. Five to fifteen. Systems, not hours.

18. Day 60 per client: results review, sign the 6 month term, pull one proof line. Add it to the offer page and the call frame. Feeds Proof.
19. Same meeting: ask for two referrals. Track the percent of new clients that came from referral. Feeds Dial and Proof.
20. At 3 Level 2 clients, or the first week response time slips past 5 minutes twice: hire a part-time First Responder. Hand over the questionnaire outputs and the Leads tab. Check their response times weekly. Feeds Lead and Capacity.
21. At 20+ hours a week building: hire a build and SEO contractor on the 14 day deadline. Feeds Capacity.
22. Dial 15 a day plus referral calls. Same log, same Friday tally. Feeds Dial.
23. Any month close rate holds above 40%: raise the price for new clients. Existing clients stay. Feeds Money.
24. Say no to custom work, including the $10k B2B job. Log every no. Feeds Capacity.

## Stage 3: mid 2027 to end of 2028. Fifteen to thirty. You stop delivering.

25. Hire an account manager who owns weekly PDFs, monthly meetings, and day 60 reviews. Your delivery hours go to zero. Feeds Capacity and Lead.
26. Each time you hand off a loop, write its SOP in `references/sops/`. Feeds every loop.
27. Monthly owner dashboard: MRR, clients lost at month 6, gross margin, owner profit, referral percent, net worth outside the business. Feeds Money.
28. Sell and hire only. Dial or run outreach at whatever cadence keeps meetings booked above target. Feeds Dial.

## Stage 4: 2029 on. Hold or sell.

29. At 30 clients and 24 months of clean books: get a valuation. Decide hold or sell. Feeds Money.
30. Hold: keep the invest percent, check net worth quarterly until it crosses $1M. Sell: at 2.5x to 3x annual profit, close, then invest the proceeds.

## Cadence

- Daily: steps 9, 15.
- Weekly: steps 10, 11, 16.
- Monthly: steps 17, 27, and the capacity check behind 20, 21, 23.
- Day 60 per client: steps 18, 19.
- Quarterly: net worth check.

## Where the AIOS takes over

Candidates for `/level-up`, in order: the Friday tally (step 10), the weekly PDF (step 16), the lead log and response timer (step 15), the month-end money loop (step 17), the owner dashboard (step 27). Each one is a manual step done 3+ times first, then automated.
